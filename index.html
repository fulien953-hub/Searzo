The Problem

Search Has Changed.
Most Brands Haven't.

AI-powered search is now how people discover and validate businesses. The old SEO playbook does not apply — and most agencies are not yet equipped to address it.

01
AI Answers Without Links

ChatGPT and Perplexity give direct answers — not a list of links. If your client's brand is not cited in those answers, it simply does not exist in the conversation.

02
Traditional SEO Is Not Enough

Ranking on Google page one no longer guarantees visibility in AI search. Only 12% of URLs cited by AI tools overlap with traditional top-10 results. It is a different game entirely.

03
The Window Is Open Now

Most competitors have not optimized for AI search yet. The agencies that move first will establish visibility for their clients before the market catches up. That window will not stay open.

04
Agencies Need a Specialist

AI search optimization requires a different skillset than traditional SEO. Rather than building it in-house, the smartest agencies partner with specialists — and deliver the results under their own brand.
